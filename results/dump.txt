frame by frame and both pad to the same period, so thermal drift and
duty differences cancel instead of being absorbed into the delta.

  The VQ delta is +0.0017 W against a 2 SE bound of 0.0146: NOT RESOLVED.
  Quote it as an UPPER BOUND -- the VQ costs less than 14.6 mW -- and
  not as zero. Three runs gave -0.0046, +0.0048 and +0.0017 W, all
  inside 2 SE. The sign is noise, and three draws show that better
  than any single one.

  MISC is 0.8060 W, 40% of the board, and is almost all VCC3V3
  (0.7450 W) -- USB, PHY, LEDs, board housekeeping. The SoC rails that
  the accelerator actually moves are PL + PS + DDR = 1.2299 W.

  energy per frame                             II ms         W        mJ
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  power loop as run (run-2 topology)         22.9631    2.0359     46.75   MEASURED
  deployed pipeline                          17.9024    2.0359     36.45   DERIVED
  serial, no overlap                         29.4885    2.0359     60.04   DERIVED
  SoC rails only, deployed                   17.9024    1.2299     22.02   DERIVED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE CAVEAT ON THE DERIVED ROWS, and it is not small.
  edge_power_measure() drives its own loop, and that loop still packs
  inside the cascade and never entropy-codes -- it is the run-2
  topology, 22.9631 ms. So the mean power above was taken while the CPU
  SPINS through the DMA waits that the deployed path now fills with
  work. Same work per frame in a 39% shorter frame, so the energy
  direction is right; the magnitude is not measured, because a busier
  CPU during those waits will draw more than a spinning one.
  Pointing the A/B at the deployed loop is the fix. It has not been
  done, so 46.75 mJ is the number to quote and 36.45 mJ is an estimate.

  regulator OUTPUT power: excludes conversion losses and the unmonitored 5 V USB rail. NOT 12 V input power.
